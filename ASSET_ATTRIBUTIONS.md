# Asset Attributions

All visual assets committed under `public/assets/blog` and `public/favicon` are
custom SVG assets created for this repository.

The previous JPEG cover images included stock-site metadata and were removed to
avoid publishing assets whose license could not be verified. Do not add
third-party photos, icons, fonts, audio, video, or generated assets unless the
source, license, and required attribution are recorded in this file.

Current assets:

- `public/assets/blog/*/cover.svg`: custom seasonal illustrations.
- `public/assets/blog/authors/zx.svg`: custom author avatar.
- `public/favicon/favicon.svg`: custom project favicon.
